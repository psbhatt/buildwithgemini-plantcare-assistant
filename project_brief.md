# My agent: PlantCare Assistant (Flora)
One-liner: A conversational agent that helps plant parents track and care for their houseplant collection with watering reminders, care logs, and photo diagnosis.

Tool coverage:
- Memory: User's home environment (light levels, indoor humidity, pet safety preferences) and past care notes.
- Tools: Log watering/fertilizing events, lookup plant care requirements, diagnose health issues from leaf photos.
- Catalog/UI: Collection of user's tracked plants rendered as A2UI status cards with watering badges and schedules.
- Image gen: Visualizing healthy plant progression or companion plant setups.
- Sandbox: Dynamic watering interval calculation based on pot volume, soil type, and season.

Recommended for every project: memory, storage, tools, image generation, A2UI
Agent-specific / stretch: Multimodal photo diagnosis with Gemini, Code sandbox for watering schedule math.
